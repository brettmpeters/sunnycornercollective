The proof

When priorities shift, keep your creators close.

TikTok · Top Creator Engagement Program
The challenge
As TikTok grew, its content priorities shifted toward categories like TV, film, sports and learning. The shift made sense for the business, and the creators who helped build the platform still needed a real place in it.
The strategy
A program built around what those creators wanted for their own careers, with a dedicated point of contact, connections to teams across the company and moments like Cannes Lions and CMO roundtables.
The result
Participating creators were measurably more confident in TikTok and more willing to speak up for it, including in Washington when the platform faced a US ban.

When a channel has potential, make it a business.

TikTok · TikTok for Good
The challenge
TikTok had a social impact account with a lot of potential and just 2,000 followers.
The strategy
Partner with ATTN: to make it the go-to place for social impact done well on TikTok, with content from creators doing standout work and support from four teams across the company.
The result
It grew to 1.3 million followers and became TikTok's first owned account to sell sponsorships, with partners including Ulta Beauty, Hilton and Maybelline.

When you're under scrutiny, let trusted voices speak.

TikTok · Change Makers
The challenge
TikTok was facing intense regulatory scrutiny around the world and needed credible voices to show the good it does for society.
The strategy
Start with the story, then find the creators already living it. The program ran in 22 countries so local teams could share stories that fit their own markets. Year two focused on professional development so creators could show up as their best selves.
The result
Creators from the program became trusted voices at Davos, UNGA and COP, speaking with a credibility no company spokesperson could. The program earned press coverage year over year, follow-ups from policymakers and a renewed budget.

When you're known for one thing, change what people see.

TikTok · Creative Learning Fund and Instructive Accelerator
The challenge
TikTok was best known for dance and lip sync, and wanted to become a place people go to learn.
The strategy
A $50 million fund for publishers, institutions and experts to create educational content. I helped launch the Creative Learning Fund, then led its second version, the Instructive Accelerator, which went deeper with fewer partners at lower cost.
The result
Within a year, #LearnOnTikTok had nearly 60 billion views, and learning became a lasting part of what TikTok is known for.

When it's a first, make it count.

It Gets Better · Doritos
The challenge
Doritos wanted to make its first move into Pride, starting with an idea for a rainbow chip. PepsiCo had never sponsored Pride before.
The strategy
Make it a limited-edition bag fans could only get by donating to It Gets Better, then work closely with Doritos and its agency over many weeks to get it approved.
The result
It sold out in under 10 hours, raised $225,000 in five days, won an Effie and became PepsiCo's first Pride sponsorship.

When you want new audiences, fund stories you don't star in.

It Gets Better · Lexus
The challenge
Lexus wanted to reach new, younger audiences through its own streaming channel.
The strategy
Three seasons of It Got Better, original programming where LGBTQ icons from Jane Lynch to Sir Ian McKellen told their own stories.
The result
Lexus saw a 187% brand lift among viewers, and season three was a Shorty Awards finalist.

About me
